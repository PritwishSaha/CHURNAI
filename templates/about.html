About ChurnAI

Machine learning that shows which customers are at risk of leaving, so you can act while there is still time.

What ChurnAI does

ChurnAI is a customer churn prediction system built on an XGBoost model. It helps teams find customers who are likely to leave, so they can plan retention work around data instead of hunches.

The model reads 10 customer details and builds four extra features from them. On test data it reaches about 83% accuracy and a ROC-AUC of about 87%.

Every result lands in one of three risk zones. A tuned threshold decides where the line sits, and the dial shows exactly where each customer falls.
